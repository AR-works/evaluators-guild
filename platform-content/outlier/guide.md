## What Is Outlier AI?

Outlier is a platform for building AI with expert human input, where freelancers help shape the next generation of AI models (Official). It is operated by Scale AI, founded in 2016 by Alexandr Wang and Lucy Guo and headquartered in San Francisco (Verified). Outlier emerged within Scale's ecosystem to meet demand for high-quality human feedback in generative AI development, particularly for fine-tuning large language models (Verified).

Unlike open microtask platforms with instant registration, Outlier targets individuals with strong educational, analytical, or technical backgrounds, and its screening reflects that (Official). Onboarding typically takes 30 to 90 minutes to complete (Official).

**Who hires through Outlier AI:** AI labs and enterprises working with Scale AI to fine-tune large language models and improve AI system outputs.

**Who should apply:** Writers and editors, artists, mathematicians, software engineers, fact-checkers across 19 specialisms, and medical professionals with relevant degrees or equivalent experience.

## Roles Offered

### AI Content Evaluator

Measures and compares the performance of different AI models by reading English text from AI models and ranking responses in order, assessing accuracy and relevance, and writing/rewriting prompts and responses (Official). Requires professional writing or editing experience, a degree in a writing-related discipline, and a strong command of English (Official). Operates on a tier system where more experienced freelancers earn more competitive rates (Official).

### Art Expert / AI Art Trainer

Trains AI models to be more accurate, relevant, and safe in AI art production by crafting and answering art-related questions, evaluating and ranking AI-generated responses, and assessing text relevance using domain expertise (Official). Requires a bachelor's or higher degree in an art-related subject, familiarity with the art landscape, strong storytelling/narrative skills, and research/fact-checking ability (Official).

### Math Trainer

Suited to professionals with quantitative expertise; crafts and answers math-related questions and evaluates/ranks domain-specific AI responses (Official).

### Coding Expert / AI Code Trainer

Recruits experienced software engineers to help train AI models to write better code, working on discrete, highly variable problems (Official). Requires a degree in computer science or higher and proficiency in Java, Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, C++, Swift, or Verilog (Official).

### Fact-Checker (19 Specialisms)

Covers fields from electronics and mechanical engineering to sociology and public health; responsibilities include training AI models by creating and answering domain questions, evaluating and ranking responses, and assessing factual accuracy (Official). Requires a bachelor's degree or higher in the relevant field and excellent English communication; some roles require five or more years of related experience (Official).

### Medical Expert

Creates clinically rigorous prompts, evaluates model responses for medical accuracy, and ensures AI reasoning meets clinical standards, fully remote (Official). Requires an MD, DO, MBBS, or equivalent (currently enrolled candidates may be considered); a research background with peer-reviewed publications is valued; clinically experienced NPs, PAs, or nursing professionals may be considered for some projects (Official).

## Hiring Process

1. **Account Creation** - Create an account at outlier.ai using email or Google sign-in; provide full name, phone number, date of birth, and address (Official).
2. **Profile Completion** - Upload an up-to-date resume and connect a LinkedIn profile; the system extracts and cross-references skills for credibility (Official).
3. **General Reasoning Screening** - A critical assessment requiring 80%+ to pass, evaluating analytical thinking; failing typically means no work is assigned (Official).
4. **Project-Specific Training & Qualification Tests** - Individual projects may require additional qualification tests or training modules before contributing (Official).
5. **Work** - No minimum hour requirements; contributors choose when and how much to work (Official).

**Timeline:** The interview/screening process takes about a week, and has been described by some as easy, though overall interview experience is rated unfavorably by some candidates (Community).

**Profile advice:** Keep the resume current and accurate, since Outlier's AI matching system relies heavily on it; only list skills that can be genuinely demonstrated, since they will be assessed (Official). Specialized knowledge in STEM fields, law, or medicine commands the highest pay rates (Official).

## Assessments

**General Reasoning Screening:** Required score of 80%+; focuses on analytical thinking and reasoning ability; failure typically means the applicant is not assigned work (Official).

**Skills tested beyond the screening:** Domain expertise (per role), writing quality and reading comprehension, logical/analytical reasoning, coding proficiency (for code roles), and medical or scientific accuracy (for expert roles) (Official).

**Application components:** A skills test and a writing sample, in addition to the reasoning screening (Official).

**Pay compression:** Outlier AI has been cutting pay through 2026; rate compression since December 2025 is documented. Projects that paid $28-$35/hr in early 2025 were restructured to $18-$22/hr by Q1 2026, driven by more qualified workers than available project capacity (Community).

**Oracle status:** Outlier's top-tier quality designation gives access to higher-paying projects and somewhat more queue stability during slow periods, though Oracle workers are still removed at the same rate as others when a project ends (Community).

**Surge pricing ("Missions"):** Contributors earn an average of 7.5% more through Missions; the top quartile boosts earnings by an average of 11% (Official).

**Income variability:** Workers report earning anywhere from $100-$1,000 in a week depending on task availability; work is generally available 9-5 weekdays but flexible (Community).

**Success stories:** An experienced evaluator with a STEM or legal background working across two premium platforms (e.g., Outlier plus Alignerr) can earn $2,500-$5,000/month at 25-30 hours/week - achievable within 6-12 months, not typical for beginners (Community).

*Note: The gap between advertised rates ($15-$50/hr), community-reported effective rates ($12-$35/hr), and Glassdoor's higher annualized figures likely reflects different measurement methods (headline vs. effective vs. full-time-equivalent). Treat the Glassdoor figures as directionally informative rather than typical contributor take-home pay.*

## Interview Tips

- **Prioritize the General Reasoning Screening.** An 80%+ score is required, and failing typically means no work is assigned - treat this as the real gatekeeper, not a formality.
- **Keep your resume and LinkedIn accurate and current.** Outlier's matching system relies heavily on these, and listed skills will be tested.
- **Only claim skills you can demonstrate.** The platform assesses claimed skills directly.
- **Lean into STEM, legal, or medical specialization if you have it.** These consistently command the highest rates.
- **Set realistic income expectations.** Treat advertised top-end rates ($50/hr+) as achievable for specialists over time, not as a starting baseline.
