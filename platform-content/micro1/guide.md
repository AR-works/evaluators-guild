## Overview

Micro1 connects professionals with real-world expertise to companies building frontier AI models. Instead of a standard job board, it uses an AI recruiter named "Zara" to screen candidates through recorded interviews and skills assessments, then certifies top performers into a talent pool that AI labs and enterprises hire from directly. It's best suited to professionals with genuine domain knowledge - in fields from engineering and healthcare to finance and linguistics - who want flexible, remote AI evaluation work and are comfortable with a proctored screening process, rather than people seeking a low-friction side gig with minimal vetting.

## What Is Micro1?

Micro1 turns real-world professional expertise into training data, evaluations, and feedback loops used to improve AI systems (Official). AI labs and enterprises use the platform to train models and build more reliable AI agents through structured evaluations and reinforcement learning environments (Official).

The company says it has raised more than $40 million in funding and that its AI recruiter has powered over 1 million AI-led interviews (Official). Its stated ambition is to become a "human intelligence layer" for AI, enabling large numbers of people to do paid work applying professional expertise to model training (Official).

Beyond text-based evaluation, Micro1 also describes itself as a contextual evaluation platform for improving AI agent performance in production, and says it has expanded into collecting real-world data to train robotics and embodied AI systems (Official).

**Who hires through Micro1:** AI labs and enterprise clients building or fine-tuning AI models, agents, and robotics systems.

**Who should apply:** Professionals across engineering, finance, healthcare/medical, legal, economics, linguistics, cinematography, and other specialized fields, from early-career to PhD/industry-leader level (Official).

## Roles Offered

### AI Evaluation Specialist

No prior AI experience required - domain knowledge is what matters (Official). Responsibilities include reviewing and critically assessing AI-generated outputs for quality, clarity, and usability; identifying inconsistencies and improvement opportunities across content types; applying structured evaluation guidelines to produce nuanced feedback; and collaborating with teams to refine quality standards and scoring criteria (Official).

### AI Model Evaluator (LLM & Agent Systems)

Aimed at analytical professionals with experience in LLM evaluation, AI output analysis, QA/testing, UX research, or similar, plus familiarity with benchmarking and rubric-based scoring (Official). Tasks include evaluating LLM and agent outputs against defined rubrics, reviewing multi-step agent actions (including screenshots and reasoning traces), flagging edge cases, and delivering structured feedback for benchmarking and model refinement (Official). Minimum requirements: English B2+ and at least 20 hours/week for the initial term (Official).

### Visual Evaluation Specialist

Focused on assessing image composition, clarity, and adherence to visual standards, with findings communicated in clear written English (Official). Requirements include proven experience in visual content evaluation or digital media, at least 15 hours/week, very high written English proficiency, strong analytical/problem-solving skills, and familiarity with digital image formats and basic visual design principles (Official).

### Other Domain Coverage

Micro1 also lists opportunities across engineering, finance, healthcare, legal, economics, linguistics, and cinematography (Official). Preferred backgrounds across roles include design, UX/UI, creative direction, editing, content strategy, or experience with annotation, QA, moderation, or AI tools/LLMs (Official).

## Hiring Process

Micro1 combines automated, skill-based assessments with human recruiter review; certification decisions are stated to be based on demonstrated knowledge rather than background, location, or personal characteristics (Official).

1. **Application** - Apply at micro1.ai/experts or via a listing on jobs.micro1.ai.
2. **Profile & Resume Upload** - Build a detailed profile of experience and skills; keeping it updated improves matching chances (Official).
3. **AI Interview with "Zara"** - Asynchronous and recorded, not live. Zara asks open-ended technical and scenario-based questions tailored to your specialization and evaluates both answers and communication clarity (Official). Candidates commonly describe it as surprisingly natural for an AI-led interaction (Community).
4. **Anti-Cheat & Technical Assessment** - Requires full desktop screen-sharing (not a single window); the assessment cannot proceed without it (Official). The session is video-first - audio and video are recorded to assess communication style and eye contact (Official).
5. **AI-Generated Assessment Report** - After voice-based Q&A and, for relevant roles, live coding, candidates receive an automated report scoring performance by skill area (Official).
6. **Certification** - Approved candidates become "Micro1 Certified" and enter the talent pool for client matching or direct application (Official).
7. **ID Verification** - Government ID upload is required. Legitimate Micro1 emails come only from @micro1.ai, @micro1.io, or @micro1.co (Official).

**Timeline:** Screening (application through technical assessment) typically takes 1-2 weeks; matching afterward can take a few days to several weeks depending on demand, specialization, and timezone (Official).

**If rejected:** Candidates can reapply after gaining more experience, and can request detailed, constructive feedback on their technical performance - a feature this dossier notes is uncommon elsewhere (Official).

**Selectivity:** Micro1 states it accepts roughly the top 1% of applicants (Official).

## Assessments

**Format:** A one-time, role-specific assessment functions as a vetting "passport" - pass it once and you're Certified into the pool client companies hire from (Official).

**Consistency:** Every candidate is asked the same questions in the same tone, removing interviewer mood or fatigue as a variable (Official).

**Question types:** Tailored to self-reported skills, combining voice-based technical interviews with coding or scenario assessments, producing a detailed evaluation report (Official).

**Human oversight:** The AI cannot unilaterally pass or fail candidates - recruiters weigh the AI assessment alongside other data points like the resume (Official).

**Reported outcomes:** Micro1 states its screening has raised the pass rate for subsequent human interviews to around 50%, versus a typical 10-15% baseline, and its CEO has estimated 80-90% of early candidate feedback was very favorable (Official). These are company-reported figures.

**Key skills tested (AI Evaluator roles):** Native/near-native English (C1/C2), strong reading comprehension, attention to detail, critical thinking, clear communication; visual/creative-adjacent roles also assess aesthetic or editorial judgment (Official).

**Preparation tips:**
- Practice explaining your reasoning aloud, not just stating the right answer - Zara evaluates communication alongside accuracy (Official).
- Use Micro1's practice tool to simulate the interview format beforehand (Official).
- Prepare case studies with measurable, specific impact (Official).
- High-demand specializations include AI/ML, cloud infrastructure, DevOps, cybersecurity, and AI training/annotation (Official).
