## What Is Alignerr?

Alignerr pays contributors to shape the future of AI by evaluating and improving AI content from anywhere, and recruits domain experts to design expert-level tasks that challenge advanced AI agents (Official). It is powered by Labelbox, a major AI data infrastructure company, and candidates are sent an onboarding email to join the Labelbox platform as part of the process (Official).

Alignerr is described as a newer entrant that emerged in 2025, recruiting AI trainers and evaluators with academic or professional backgrounds and positioning itself as a premium network (Official). It advertises pay up to $150/hr for aligning AI models with a contributor's expertise (Official).

**Who hires through Alignerr:** AI labs and enterprises working with Labelbox's data infrastructure to train and evaluate AI models and agents.

**Who should apply:** Generalists wanting structured evaluation tasks, search quality evaluators, software engineers with modern AI/LLM tooling experience, video annotators, language experts (e.g., Chinese language specialists), and accounting experts.

## Roles Offered

### AI Training Specialist

A fully remote, flexible contract role completing structured evaluation tasks - labeling, classifying, and rating AI-generated content across text, image, and audio formats (Official). No prior AI or technical experience is required; the role is open to motivated individuals from any background who can follow detailed guidelines (Official). Work is asynchronous and self-managed (Official).

### Search Quality Evaluator

Assesses and rates search engine results, AI-generated answers, and recommendations to improve accuracy and usefulness (Official). No technical background is required - Alignerr looks for good judgment, attention to detail, and strong web browsing skills (Official). The application process is designed to take approximately 10-15 minutes (Official).

### Software Engineer / AI Code Evaluator

Involves working across multiple languages and codebases to assess generalization and correctness (Official). Requires 3-4+ years of professional software engineering experience and strong proficiency in at least one of TypeScript, Ruby, Java, or C++, plus excellent written and spoken English (Official). Candidates must reason about complex systems, debug non-obvious issues, and be familiar with modern AI/LLM tooling (Git, CLI workflows, testing frameworks) (Official).

### Video Annotation

Contributors annotate and label video content to help train AI to "see," for cutting-edge AI projects (Official).

### Language / Linguistics Roles

Alignerr recruits Chinese language experts (native speaker teaching experience welcome) and video content annotators for AI projects (Official).

### Accounting Expert (AI Agent Training)

Designs expert-level reconciliation tasks that challenge advanced AI agents, training the next generation of AI in accounting reasoning (Official).

## Hiring Process

1. **Sign Up** - Sign in with a Google account; can be completed on desktop or mobile, with Chrome recommended (Official).
2. **Resume Upload** - Upload a resume; the platform auto-populates the profile in about 30 seconds, after which the file can be deleted (Official).
3. **Profile Review & Skills Selection** - Review auto-filled details, correct location and other info, and select skill/domain preferences that guide job matching (Official).
4. **Alignerr Connect (Optional)** - Opt in or out of Alignerr Connect, which provides additional opportunities to work directly with leading AI labs (Official).
5. **Job-Specific Requirements** - Some jobs require a Zara AI interview or domain-specific assessment; applicants are notified if this applies. Engineering-role interviews test fundamentals of React, TypeScript, and Python (Official).
6. **ID Verification** - Government ID required after the AI interview for verification, with a contract signed early in onboarding (Official).
7. **Contract & Payment Setup** - Once approved, applicants receive an invitation to join the Alignerr community and complete contract and billing setup via Stripe Connect or PayPal (Official).

**Timeline:** The hiring process averages 28 days across all job titles, based on user-submitted interview data (Community).

**Application review:** Alignerr reviews applications daily, so completing all steps promptly matters for being considered (Official).

## Assessments

**Zara AI interview:** Some roles require a Zara AI interview or domain-specific assessment as an additional step before starting (Official).

**Community sentiment on difficulty:** Interview questions themselves are described as reasonable, but later assessments are reported as harder than the initial resume screen suggests (Community).

**Transparency concerns:** Alignerr aggressively publishes hiring announcements on LinkedIn without clearly disclosing upfront that the hiring process is entirely AI-led; there are also community concerns about how interview data is used, with the AI chatbot reportedly confirming it uses user data to train AI models (Community).

**Code Evaluator assessment focus:** For Software Engineer/AI Code Evaluator roles, automated interviews test knowledge of React, TypeScript, Python, Ruby, Java, and C++ (Official).

**Preparation advice:** Tailor your resume to highlight web browsing, critical thinking, and written communication for search-quality roles; prepare for tasks that test judgment and attention to detail; complete all application steps quickly since reviews happen daily (Official).

## Community Experiences

## Who Should Consider Alignerr?

Entry-level applicants wanting a fast, low-friction way into AI evaluation work (Search Quality Evaluator, AI Training Specialist), and specialists in coding, accounting, or language who want access to the platform's higher pay ceiling.

## Who Should Skip This Platform?

- Anyone who wants full transparency into an AI-led hiring process upfront - the process being entirely AI-driven is not clearly disclosed in Alignerr's public announcements (Community).
- Contributors who need consistent, predictable ongoing work - reports of silent account deletions and long idle periods suggest inconsistent project continuity (Community).
- Applicants uncomfortable with their interview/interaction data potentially being used to train AI models (Community).
- Anyone expecting the advertised $150/hr ceiling as a typical rate - community-reported general rates run closer to $15-$60/hr (Community).
